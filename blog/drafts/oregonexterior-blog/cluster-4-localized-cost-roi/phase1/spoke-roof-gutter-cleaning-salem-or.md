## What You're Actually Paying For

A roof cleaning isn't cosmetic. Here's the physics.

Salem sits on Willamette Silt. Beneath that, the Woodburn clay series — a soil with seasonal shrink-swell behavior. During wet months, the clay expands. During August and September, it contracts. This cycle stresses foundations, and foundation stress transfers upward through framing connections to your roof structure.

Why does this matter for roof cleaning?

Because moss and debris on your roof add dead load. On a 2,500 sq ft roof, a mature moss layer weighs 400–800 lbs. That's not evenly distributed — it concentrates in valleys, along eaves, and behind penetrations (vents, skylights, chimneys). Combined with the micro-movements from shrink-swell soil beneath, you get accelerated wear at fastening points and flashing terminations.

A roof cleaning in Salem isn't just removing moss. It's removing load from a structure that's already under geological stress.

Salem Revised Code Ch. 75 governs stormwater management at the residential level. Your gutters aren't just gutters — they're part of your lot's stormwater conveyance system. When they're clogged, water sheets off the roof edge, saturates the soil at your foundation perimeter, and amplifies the shrink-swell cycle that's already working against your footing.

The $150–$280 gutter cleaning isn't maintenance. It's foundation insurance.

## When to Schedule (Salem-Specific)

Timing matters more in the Valley than in Portland. Here's the Salem calendar:

- **February–March:** Optimal for moss treatment. Moss is dormant but still attached. Zinc sulfate penetrates deeply before spring growth. Best pricing — contractors haven't filled spring schedules yet.
- **April–May:** Pollen and silt season begins. Clean gutters before pollen drop peaks. Roof cleaning after pollen season ends (late May) is more effective but harder to schedule.
- **June–August:** Dry season. Moss is inactive. Good window for zinc strip installation (adhesive bonds better in dry conditions). Gutter cleaning is less urgent but proactive before fall.
- **September–October:** Critical window. Clean gutters and flush downspouts before the first heavy rains. This is the single most important maintenance event for protecting Salem foundations from shrink-swell amplification.
- **November–January:** Emergency only. Working on wet roofs increases labor cost by 20–30% and limits treatment effectiveness.

**The short version:** Book moss treatment in February–March. Book gutter service in September. Combine them in March for the best package price.
