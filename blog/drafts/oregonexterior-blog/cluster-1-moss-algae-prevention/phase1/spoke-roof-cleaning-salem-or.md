## Why Salem Costs Less (and Why That Can Be Misleading)

Salem roofs cost less to clean for three mechanical reasons:

**1. Lower average pitch.** South Salem neighborhoods — the blocks between Bush's Pasture Park and Sunnyside — are dominated by ranch-style and split-level homes from the 1960s–1980s. Average pitch is 4:12 to 6:12. Lower pitch means less fall protection equipment, faster coverage rate, and lower labor cost per square foot.

**2. Lighter average moss load.** Salem's Willamette Valley floor position gives it less tree canopy density than Portland's hill neighborhoods. The mix is valley oaks, Oregon ash, and ornamental species — fewer evergreen conifers dropping acidic needles year-round. Agricultural buffer zones around Salem's perimeter reduce canopy further. Moss grows slower here. Average treatment depth is 60%–70% of what we see in Portland's Eastmoreland or West Hills.

**3. Simpler access.** Ranch homes are one story. Most Salem roofs can be serviced entirely from ground-level or single-story ladder sets. Portland's multi-story Craftsmen and hillside construction routinely require multi-section ladder systems, roof harness setups, and longer hose runs.

But here's the catch. Salem's soil — Willamette Silt over Woodburn clay series — has seasonal shrink-swell behavior. This causes subtle foundation movement that translates to roof plane shifts over time. Flashing details and valley geometry on Salem homes are more likely to have micro-gaps that moss exploits. Cheaper cleaning doesn't mean lower risk. It means the damage vectors are different.

## The 2026 ORSC Factor

The 2026 Oregon Residential Specialty Code applies statewide — Salem and Portland equally. The code tightens performance standards for roof assemblies. Salem Revised Code Chapter 75 adds stormwater requirements that parallel Portland's Title 24 overlay.

What this means for pricing: contractors who follow the 2026 ORSC properly will charge more than those who don't. Stormwater containment during soft wash, neutralization of chemical runoff, documentation of roof condition — these add time and cost. A quote that's $200 below everyone else is cutting corners somewhere. Usually it's stormwater handling or documentation.

The Willamette Greenway overlay in Salem adds restrictions for properties near the river. If your South Salem home borders the Willamette, expect a 10%–15% premium for Greenway-compliant wash procedures.

## How to Get an Accurate Quote

Three things determine your actual price. Have these ready when you call:

1. **Roof square footage and pitch.** Check your original building plans or measure from the ground. Pitch matters more than square footage for cost — a 1,500 sq ft roof at 10:12 costs more than a 2,500 sq ft roof at 4:12.
2. **Moss coverage estimate.** Look at your north-facing slopes. 0%–25% is light, 25%–50% is moderate, 50%+ is heavy. Each tier adds roughly $75–$125 to the base price.
3. **Access conditions.** Single story, clear ground access = base price. Multi-story, landscaping obstacles, or steep lot grade = add 15%–25%.
